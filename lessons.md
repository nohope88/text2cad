# text2cad lessons (auto-maintained — repair sessions append here)
# A lesson that repeats must GRADUATE to code: a golden block, a gate linter
# check, or a brief-template constraint. Graduated lessons are marked [GRADUATED].

- Always fuse every piece into ONE solid with a single union chain, and make each piece OVERLAP its neighbors by >= 1mm — tangent/touching faces do NOT fuse and produce multiple bodies (Huggy 2026-08-10: 13 bodies, not watertight).
- Before exporting, verify in the cad tool JSON that the part is a single connected watertight solid (bodies == 1); never export on "no error" alone.
- [GRADUATED → gate bridge_span check] A wide flat internal ceiling (sealed-chamber roof) is an unsupported bridge that aggregate overhang% cannot see — what matters is local SPAN (inscribed-circle diameter of each down-facing patch), not area: a 16mm slot bridges fine, a 50mm chamber roof sags. Gate now fails span > 25mm (eclipse-v2 printability lens 2026-08-11; retro-caught eclipse-v1 at 52.7mm and validated robot at 23mm).
- [GRADUATED → BUILD prompt numeric-contract rule] Every explicit number the brief states must become an assert in params.py validate() — the build used a 6mm fillet where the brief demanded >=10mm twice (eclipse-v2 fidelity lens 2026-08-11).
- [GRADUATED → DRAFT prompt hero-legibility rule] A hero render a stranger cannot decode in 3 seconds fails sellability regardless of geometry quality — orient defining features toward the camera and make every key feature visible in at least one render (eclipse-v2 sellability lens 2026-08-11).
- [GRADUATED → gate lint + blocks/cadblocks.rounded_box] Never fillet ALL three edge directions of a box (top + bottom + vertical) at any single corner — whether via one blanket `.edges().fillet(r)` or several chained per-selector fillets that together still cover every corner. OCCT must synthesize a spherical vertex-blend there, and in this environment that blend tessellates to a degenerate zero-area sliver triangle disconnected from the main mesh (~8 phantom bodies per fully-filleted box), passing `is_solid`/no-error checks yet failing the watertight/bodies gate — reproduced with a bare `cq.Workplane("XY").box(10,10,10).edges().fillet(1)`, so it's not fixable by adjusting radius, order, or mesh tolerance. Fix: bake vertical-corner rounding into the 2D profile (arcs via a rounded-rect wire, then `.extrude()`) so no sharp vertex remains, and apply any additional top/bottom-only fillets afterward — those two edge loops never share a vertex with each other so no vertex-blend is ever built (Huggy 2026-08-11 repair).
- A sealed cavity (only a pinhole/bore for access, no post-print reach-in) must never have a flat internal ceiling wider than the safe bridge span — bake the cavity's 2D profile with a peaked "tent" top (two <=45deg roof slopes meeting at a center ridge at the same max height as the old flat top) instead of a flat-topped rounded rect; this drops bridge_span_mm to 0 while leaving the exterior shell and every brief-stated dimension untouched (eclipse-v2 printability repair 2026-08-11, fixing the finding gate.py's bridge_span check now catches automatically). Separately: if a fillet on an exterior seam starts failing with OCCT `StdFail_NotDone` after an unrelated cavity-cut/union change elsewhere in the same solid (even at a radius that worked before), apply that fillet to the raw exterior union BEFORE the cut/union chain, not after — the simpler pre-cut topology is far more likely to solve at the same radius.
- When a brief states two dimensions that geometrically trade off (an appended limb's own length/angle vs. a required clear zone on the trunk it attaches to), check feasibility analytically at brief-authoring time, before build: for a limb revolved/rotated off axis at angle `kink` with length `L` and end radius `r`, its worst-case backward reach over the trunk is `L*cos(180-kink) + r*sin(180-kink)` (endpoint values only — no taper-curve shape or fillet trick changes this, it's fixed by the limb's own stated length/angle/tip-radius). eclipse-v2 (2026-08-11): a 55mm/55deg eyepiece branch with a 34mm hood mathematically reaches back ~46mm over the tube no matter how it's built, leaving only 29 of the brief's required 50mm grip span — two repair passes maxed out every legal construction lever (hood OD at spec floor, junction pinned to the validate() floor next to the screen) and could only buy back 4mm, because the true limiting terms are the limb's own brief-mandated length/angle/tip-size, not the construction. A "change geometry construction only, do not redesign" repair session cannot close a gap that traces back to two explicit, mutually-conflicting brief numbers — that must be caught (and one number relaxed, or the limb shortened) when the brief is written, not discovered post-build.
